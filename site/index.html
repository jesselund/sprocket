For money service businesses

Stop pre-funding. Fund on demand.

To pay out quickly, MSBs have traditionally parked capital in pre-funded accounts in every corridor they serve. That money sits idle, has to be watched and topped up, and limits how fast you can grow. Sprocket funds each payment on demand using stablecoins as the settlement rail, so your capital stays working instead of waiting in nostro accounts.

Pre-funded model

  • Capital locked in balances in every corridor
  • Constant monitoring and manual top-ups
  • Payouts stall when a balance runs low
  • Each new corridor needs more capital

Sprocket fund-on-demand

  • Each payment funded at the moment it's sent
  • No balances to monitor or replenish
  • Stablecoin settlement moves value in real time
  • Add corridors without adding idle capital
